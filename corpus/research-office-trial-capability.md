# Clinical Research Office Capability Brief: Neurodegenerative Disease

## Current portfolio
- 4 active dementia-related studies: 2 industry-sponsored phase 3 trials, 1 NIH-funded observational cohort and 1 caregiver-intervention study.
- 3.0 FTE research coordinators support the neurology portfolio, shared with movement disorders.
- 1 principal investigator in behavioral neurology. Two more neurologists are interested in sub-investigator roles.

## Infrastructure
- Amyloid PET through the partner imaging center, under a research rate agreement.
- On-site MRI with research protocol support. Scheduling competes with clinical demand.
- Central IRB reliance agreements in place, which cut study start-up time to about 90 days.
- No on-site lumbar puncture clinic for research CSF collection yet.

## Gaps for sponsor feasibility
Sponsors running phase 3 anti-tau and prevention trials typically ask for:
1. Dedicated research MRI capacity with fast safety reads.
2. Access to a pre-screened pool of patients with biomarker-confirmed early Alzheimer's disease.
3. At least 2 experienced coordinators per active phase 3 trial.
4. An infusion capacity commitment for investigational IV products.

## Opportunity
A clinical anti-amyloid program would create a biomarker-confirmed, MRI-monitored patient population and infusion workflows. Those are the same capabilities sponsors screen for. Adding 2 coordinators and protected research MRI slots would make Northlake competitive for 2 to 3 more industry trials a year.

## Ask
Fund 2 research coordinator FTEs and a 0.5 FTE regulatory specialist in FY2027.
