# Infusion Center Capacity Review, Q3 2026

## Current capacity
- Two infusion centers, North Campus (22 chairs) and West Campus (16 chairs), for 38 chairs in total.
- Both centers operate 12 hours a day, about 250 days a year, which is roughly 3,000 chair-hours per chair per year.
- System-wide chair utilization in Q3 2026 was 86%. North Campus ran at 91% and West Campus at 79%.
- Oncology accounts for 64% of chair-hours. Immunology and neurology account for the rest.

## Anti-amyloid demand model
Anti-amyloid antibodies for early Alzheimer's disease are given by IV infusion. Lecanemab is infused every two weeks and donanemab every four weeks. Each visit includes infusion time plus post-infusion observation.

Planning assumptions (internal estimates):
- 140 treated patients in year one, split evenly between the two products.
- Lecanemab: about 26 visits per patient per year at 2 chair-hours each, for 52 chair-hours per patient-year.
- Donanemab: about 13 visits per patient per year at 2.5 chair-hours each, for 32.5 chair-hours per patient-year.
- Total year-one demand: about 5,900 chair-hours, the equivalent of 2 dedicated chairs at full use.

## Findings
- North Campus cannot absorb the new demand without displacing oncology.
- West Campus has enough slack for about 2 chairs today. Dedicating 3 chairs gives headroom for growth and no-shows.
- Each anti-amyloid visit needs a nurse trained in infusion-reaction management, and a protocol to hold dosing when a safety MRI shows ARIA.

## Recommendation
Dedicate 3 chairs at West Campus to neurology infusions starting Q1 FY2027. Reassess at 80% utilization of those chairs.
