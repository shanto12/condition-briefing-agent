# Memory Clinic Referral and Diagnosis Analytics, FY2026

## Volume and access
- 2,430 new referrals in FY2026, up 22% from 1,990 in FY2025.
- Referral sources: primary care 71%, emergency and inpatient 12%, self or family 9%, other specialists 8%.
- The median wait from referral to a new memory clinic visit is 94 days, against a 30-day target.
- The neuropsychological testing backlog is 11 weeks.

## Diagnoses at first evaluation (completed evaluations, n=1,860)
| Diagnosis | Share |
|---|---|
| Alzheimer's disease (MCI or dementia stage) | 58% |
| Vascular dementia | 14% |
| Mixed or other dementia | 17% |
| Dementia with Lewy bodies | 7% |
| Frontotemporal dementia | 4% |

About 36% of Alzheimer's diagnoses were at the mild cognitive impairment (MCI) or mild dementia stage. That is the population where anti-amyloid therapy may apply.

## Biomarker testing
- Amyloid PET: 310 scans, through a partner imaging center.
- Cerebrospinal fluid (CSF) amyloid and tau: 120 tests.
- Blood biomarker pilot: 400 plasma p-tau217 tests, used to triage who needs PET or CSF confirmation.

## Payer mix (memory clinic visits)
Medicare fee-for-service 41%, Medicare Advantage 33%, commercial 18%, Medicaid 6%, self-pay 2%.

## Takeaways
Demand is rising faster than diagnostic capacity. Blood-based triage and more neuropsychology capacity would shorten the path to treatment decisions more than any other single change.
