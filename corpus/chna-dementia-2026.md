# Community Health Needs Assessment Excerpt: Dementia and Aging

## Service area population
- Northlake Health's six-county service area has about 412,000 residents aged 65 and older.
- The 65-and-older population is projected to grow 18% by 2032.
- National estimates put Alzheimer's dementia at about 1 in 9 adults aged 65 and older. Applied to our service area, that suggests roughly 45,000 residents living with Alzheimer's dementia, many of them undiagnosed.

## Access gaps
- 23% of residents aged 65 and older live more than 60 minutes' drive from a memory clinic, concentrated in the two rural western counties.
- Community focus groups named three barriers: long waits for diagnosis, transportation to repeated appointments, and caregiver burnout.
- Spanish-language memory care resources are limited. 9% of older adults in the service area speak Spanish at home.

## Caregiver burden
Most people living with dementia are cared for at home by unpaid family caregivers. Survey respondents reported high stress and limited access to respite care.

## Priorities identified
1. Shorten time to diagnosis, especially for rural residents.
2. Expand caregiver education and respite.
3. Offer telehealth follow-up to reduce travel for monitoring visits.
4. Grow bilingual navigation and education materials.

## Implication for service-line planning
Any infusion-based therapy program needs transportation support and rural-friendly scheduling. Patients may need visits every two to four weeks for months.
