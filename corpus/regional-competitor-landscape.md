# Regional Competitor Landscape: Memory Care

## Lakeshore Neuro Institute (academic medical center)
- Comprehensive memory center with behavioral neurology, neuropsychology and an active Alzheimer's research program.
- Launched an anti-amyloid infusion program in 2024. It has 6 dedicated chairs and reports a waitlist for evaluation.
- Site for several industry-sponsored phase 3 trials, including anti-tau and next-generation anti-amyloid studies.
- Draws referrals from our northern ZIP codes. We estimate 180 to 220 of our referred patients a year go there for evaluation.

## Prairie Valley Health (community health system)
- General neurology only, with no dedicated memory clinic and no infusion program for anti-amyloid therapy.
- Sends complex cognitive referrals to Lakeshore and occasionally to Northlake.
- Possible partner: a referral agreement, or a shared navigator model for its rural patients.

## Meridian Memory Partners (independent neurology group)
- Private practice with 4 neurologists, offering blood biomarker testing and amyloid PET through an imaging partner.
- Started anti-amyloid infusions in 2025 at a freestanding infusion suite with 4 chairs.
- Competes on speed: it advertises new patient visits within 30 days.

## Implications for Northlake
1. We are the only large system in the western counties without an anti-amyloid program.
2. Speed to diagnosis is the main competitive gap. Meridian promises 30 days, and our median is 94 days.
3. Trial participation is a differentiator Lakeshore already holds. Our research office could close part of that gap.
4. Prairie Valley is a partnership opportunity for rural reach.
