# Neurosciences Workforce Plan: Dementia Care

## Current staffing (FTE)
| Role | Current FTE |

| Infusion nurses trained for neurology infusions | 4.0 |

## Gaps
- The neuropsychology backlog is 11 weeks. One added neuropsychologist would cut it to about 5 weeks.
- New patient capacity is about 1,500 evaluations a year, against 2,430 referrals.
- An anti-amyloid program adds care coordination and monitoring work for about 140 treated patients.

## Hiring plan for an anti-amyloid program
1. 2 neurologists, with behavioral neurology preferred. Recruitment usually takes 9 to 12 months.
2. 1 neuropsychologist.
3. 2 nurse navigators. Pathway guidance is 1 navigator per 60 to 70 active patients.
4. 1 prior-authorization specialist for neurology infusion.
5. 4 more infusion nurses trained in infusion-reaction and ARIA symptom recognition.

## Alternatives while recruiting
- Advanced practice providers (nurse practitioners or physician assistants) for follow-up visits, freeing neurologists for new evaluations.
- eConsults with primary care to manage referral appropriateness.
- Shared neuropsychology testing with a technician model.

## Cost note
The fully loaded annual cost of the hiring plan is about $2.4M. Positions are phased over 18 months.
