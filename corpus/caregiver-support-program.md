# Dementia Caregiver Support Program Brief

## Current services
- 2.0 FTE memory care social workers handle caregiver needs assessments and community referrals.
- Monthly caregiver education sessions, about 35 attendees each, in person and virtual.
- A respite referral network with 5 adult day programs and 2 in-home care agencies.
- No 24/7 caregiver support line today.

## CMS GUIDE Model
The CMS Guiding an Improved Dementia Experience (GUIDE) Model is a Medicare payment model, launched in 2024, for comprehensive dementia care. It covers care coordination, caregiver training and support, a 24/7 support line, and respite services for eligible beneficiaries.

**Northlake status:** we have not yet joined. The Strategy Office recommends applying in the next available cohort. GUIDE payments could fund navigators, the support line and respite for traditional Medicare patients. They make up 41% of memory clinic visits.

## Program gaps
1. No standardized caregiver burden screening at memory clinic visits.
2. Limited Spanish-language education. 9% of older adults in the service area speak Spanish at home.
3. Respite capacity is thin in the two rural western counties.

## Proposed FY2027 enhancements
- Add a caregiver burden screen at every memory clinic follow-up.
- Launch a 24/7 support line, contracted or in-house, aligned with GUIDE requirements.
- Hire 1 bilingual care navigator.
- Expand respite partnerships to the rural counties.

## Link to the anti-amyloid program
Patients on infusion therapy rely on care partners for transportation to frequent infusions and MRIs, and for watching for symptoms of ARIA. Caregiver support is a prerequisite for a safe program, not an add-on.
